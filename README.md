# Rohit Sharma Tribute Webpage 🏏

A clean, responsive, and elegant tribute webpage dedicated to **Rohit Sharma** ("The Hitman"), Indian cricket legend and 2024 T20 World Cup winning captain.

## 🌟 Project Features

- **Hero Header**: Highlighting Rohit Sharma's key achievements and world records.
- **Featured Photo & Bio**: Dedicated HD portrait card featuring Jersey #45 details and bio.
- **Career Statistics**: Key milestones including 19,000+ international runs and 48 centuries.
- **Career Journey Timeline**: Chronological milestones from his 2007 debut to the 2024 T20 World Cup triumph.
- **Batting Mastery**: Overview of his signature pull shot, lazy elegance, and tactical leadership.
- **Honors & Awards**: Highlighting the Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna, Arjuna Award, and ICC awards.
- **Major Records**: 3 ODI double centuries, most international sixes, and 5 WC centuries in a single edition.
- **Inspirational Quotes**: Dedicated section featuring iconic quotes by Rohit Sharma.

## 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML5**: Clean, semantic markup.
- **CSS3**: Pure, basic, and responsive stylesheet (No heavy frameworks or libraries).
